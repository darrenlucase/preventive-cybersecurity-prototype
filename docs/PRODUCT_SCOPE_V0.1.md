# Product Scope V0.1

## Purpose

The unnamed prototype demonstrates preventive protection before a deceptive website interaction exposes credentials or personal data. Portugal is the first community and research context; the technical architecture is reusable internationally.

## In scope

- English and European Portuguese consumer experience.
- URL decomposition and controlling-domain explanation.
- Deterministic fictional reputation provider.
- Fictional verified-organisation registry and claimed-identity comparison.
- Transparent lookalike, misleading-subdomain, and punycode signals.
- Deterministic risk states and evidence quality.
- Reversible browser-local block and trust actions with saved-state verification.
- Five controlled scenarios and non-submitting sensitive-field demonstrations.
- Methodology and teacher project pages.

## Out of scope

Antivirus, endpoint monitoring, live browsing interception, authentication, accounts, analytics, real browsing history, real credential collection, exploit or offensive capability, and real-world safety certification.
