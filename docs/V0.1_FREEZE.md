## Frozen capabilities

URL/domain parsing, controlled registrable-domain logic, HTTPS and punycode detection, provider abstraction, deterministic fictional reputation, claimed-identity comparison, similarity signals, sensitive-context warnings, explainable risk states, reversible local block/trust actions, action verification, nine controlled scenarios, bilingual UI, methodology and teacher presentation material.

## Known limitations

The reputation dataset and organisations are fictional. The public-suffix implementation is deliberately limited. No result proves a website safe. Similarity can produce false positives or false negatives. Blocking works only inside the prototype. No real-world effectiveness claim is made.

The controlled evaluation recorded one low-severity design limitation: block and trust are independent lists, so the same domain can appear in both. No production behavior was changed during evaluation.

## Deferred

Live external threat intelligence, a real verified-organisation registry, browser extension, real-world interception or blocking, privacy/tracker protection, breach monitoring, account remediation, call/SMS monitoring, AI verdicts, accounts and databases.
